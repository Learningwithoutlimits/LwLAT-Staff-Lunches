How access is worked out

First match wins:

  1. Built-in admins: the app's owners plus the ADMIN_EMAILS setting in Netlify (always overall admin, listed below)
  2. Manual overrides for people listed here. Automatic entries keep following the defaults until edited.
  3. Entra groups
  4. Central domains (all sites)
  5. Site domains (set on the Sites tab). By default, lwlat.org.uk gets LwLAT Central only.

Sites

The 5 academies and the trust (LwLAT Central), and the staff email domain for each one.

How site domains work

Anyone signing in on a site's email domain gets that site automatically, e.g. @babington.lwlat.org.uk → Babington. Domains match exactly: babington.lwlat.org.uk never counts as lwlat.org.uk.

Before you add a group

Group rules need the groups claim turned on in the Entra app registration (Token configuration → Add groups claim → Security groups). Use the group's object ID, not its name.
