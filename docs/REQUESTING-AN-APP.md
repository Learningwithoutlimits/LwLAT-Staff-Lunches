# Requesting a new web app from IT Services

IT Services builds LwLAT web apps from a standard template. Every app gets Microsoft sign-in, the LwLAT Pulse look, an admin area, backups and a security review as standard, so you **don't need to design the look or ask for those**. What we need from you is what the app is for, who uses it, and what information it holds.

Email the request to **itservices@lwlat.org.uk** with the subject **"New app request: <name>"**, using the form below. Short answers are fine. If you don't know an answer, say so and we'll work it out with you.

## What happens next

1. **We reply** within a week to talk it through and agree what's in the first version.
2. **Data protection:** if the app holds personal information, we check it with the Data Protection Officer before building. This can take a couple of weeks, so the more detail you give in section 4, the quicker it goes.
3. **IT Services sets up** the Microsoft sign-in (Entra), hosting and any email or system connections. You don't need to do anything technical.
4. **You test it** with a few colleagues, using made-up data.
5. **Go live:** we add it to LwLAT Pulse and give access to the people or groups you named.

## What you'll get as standard

- Sign-in with your normal LwLAT Microsoft account, with no extra passwords.
- Access by academy or role: for example, each academy sees only its own records, and an admin sees everything.
- The LwLAT Pulse look, a link back to Pulse, light and dark mode, and accessibility settings.
- An admin area: who has access, a log of every change, and who signed in.
- Export to a spreadsheet, and print-friendly pages.
- A **Feedback** button for reporting problems and ideas.
- Daily backups, and IT Services is told automatically if something breaks.

## Request form (copy into your email)

```
NEW APP REQUEST

1. About the app
   Name (short, e.g. "Visitor Log"):
   Which word in the name should be highlighted? (e.g. "Log"):
   What problem does it solve? What do people do today instead (spreadsheet, paper, email)?
   What are the 3–5 main things people need to do in it?
   -
   -
   -

2. Who uses it
   Which sites? (Babington, South Wigston, Lancaster, Woodstock, Heatherbrook, LwLAT Central, or all)
   Who uses it, roughly how many people, and what can each group do?
     e.g. Reception staff (about 10): add and close visitors at their own academy
     e.g. Site managers (5): see everything at their academy, run reports
     e.g. Trust leads (3): see every academy
   Who should be the app's owners/admins? (at least two people, with emails)
   Is there a Microsoft group or distribution list that already holds these people?

3. What it records
   What information does each record hold? (list the fields, e.g. visitor name, company, time in, time out)
   Any files or photos?
   Does anything need to be printed? What does the printout look like?
   Should it import anything (e.g. an existing spreadsheet)?

4. Personal information
   Does it hold information about people? Whose (staff, visitors, contractors, parents, pupils)?
   Anything sensitive (health, allergies, safeguarding, HR, financial)?
   PUPIL INFORMATION: yes / no. If yes, what and why?
   How long should records be kept? (e.g. "end of the school year", "5 years")
   Who else will see this information outside the app, if anyone?

5. Notifications and connections
   Should it email anyone when something happens? Who, and when?
   Does it need to talk to another system? (Freshservice, Sign In App, Paxton, SIMS data, Microsoft Teams, other)

6. Timing and support
   When do you need it by, and why that date?
   Who will look after the information in it day to day (add users, check records)?
   Anything else we should know?
```

## Tips for a good request

- **Describe the job, not the screens.** "Reception needs to see who's on site in a fire drill" is more useful than a drawing of a page.
- **Send a copy of what you use now** (with made-up names), such as the spreadsheet or paper form. It's the quickest way for us to understand the fields.
- **Never send real personal data**, especially pupil data, in the request or in example files.
- **Start small.** A first version that does the main job well can go live sooner; extras can follow.
- **Don't worry about the look.** All LwLAT apps share the Pulse design so they feel familiar. Tell us only the name and the highlighted word.
